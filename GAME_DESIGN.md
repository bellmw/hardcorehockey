# Hockey GM — Game Design Document

## Overview

A single-player hockey general manager simulation. You are the GM — never the
coach. All games simulate automatically. Your job is to build a roster, manage
the cap, respond to trade offers, make draft picks, and survive relegation.

---

## Leagues

Three leagues of 12 teams each. Fictional names, real Canadian and American cities.

### Premier Hockey League (PHL) — Tier 1
The big show. Higher player quality, bigger contracts, more scrutiny.

### Continental Division (CD) — Tier 2
Competitive but scrappy. Teams hover between glory and relegation.

### Regional Circuit (RC) — Tier 3
The basement. Talent is raw. Budgets are thin. Events are weird.

---

## Promotion & Relegation

At the end of every season:

```
Top 2 teams   → auto-promoted to the league above
Bottom 2 teams → auto-relegated to the league below
3rd place vs 10th place → one-game survival playoff
  Winner: survives (stays in league)
  Loser: drops (relegated or misses promotion)
```

PHL has no league above. RC has no league below.
Teams finishing 1st or 2nd in PHL win the championship (1st = Cup, 2nd = Silver).

---

## Season Structure

### Regular Season
- 20 games per season
- Each team plays every other team in their league at least once
- 2 points for a win, 1 for OT/SO loss, 0 for regulation loss
- Standings: Points → Wins → Goal differential

### Playoffs
- Top 4 teams in each league qualify
- Single-elimination bracket: (1 vs 4), (2 vs 3), winners meet in final
- Each round is a single simulated game (no series)
- Playoff results do NOT affect promotion/relegation standings

### Off-Season Order
1. Playoff results announced
2. Relegation / promotion determined
3. Player contracts expire (see Contracts)
4. Free agency window opens
5. Draft held (3 rounds)
6. New season begins

## Player System

### Positions
- C — Centre
- LW — Left Wing
- RW — Right Wing
- LD — Left Defence
- RD — Right Defence
- G — Goalie

### Roster Size
- 20 players minimum, 25 maximum
- Active game roster: 12 forwards (4 lines of 3), 6 defence (3 pairs), 2 goalies

### Player Attributes
Each player has a single **Overall Rating** (1–99) that determines their value,
plus a hidden variance that makes each game slightly unpredictable.

Forwards also have a **Shooting** sub-rating (affects goal probability).
Defensemen have a **Physical** sub-rating (affects opponent shooting).
Goalies have a **Save%** sub-rating (affects shots-on-goal conversion).

### Player Generation
Players are generated with:
```js
{
  id: uuid,
  firstName: string,
  lastName: string,
  position: 'C' | 'LW' | 'RW' | 'LD' | 'RD' | 'G',
  overall: 40–99,
  age: 18–36,
  salary: 500000–6000000,     // based on overall
  contractYears: 1–4,         // years remaining
  nationality: 'CAN' | 'USA', // flavour only
  trait: string               // one personality trait, e.g. "hothead" "clutch" "glass jaw"
}
```

### Aging
- At the end of each season all players age by 1 year
- Players aged 35+ have a 20% chance of retiring
- Players aged 18–24 have a 10% chance of improving by 1–3 overall
- Players aged 29–34 have a 15% chance of declining by 1–2 overall

---

## Contracts & Salary Cap

### Salary Cap
Starts at $40,000,000. Increases by $2,000,000 each season.

### Contracts
- Every player has a salary and years remaining
- When `contractYears` reaches 0, the player becomes a free agent
- You cannot exceed the cap at season start (hard cap)
- You can be over cap mid-season only if a player retires or is released

### Releasing Players
- Any player can be released at any time (cut from roster)
- Released players become free agents instantly
- Dead cap penalty: 25% of remaining contract salary counts against cap for 1 season

### Free Agency
- Happens in the off-season window
- Available free agents listed with: name, position, overall, age, asking salary, contract length
- First come first served — sign them before rival AI GMs do
- Rival GMs sign free agents automatically during the window

---

## Draft

### Structure
- 3 rounds, 36 picks per round (one per team)
- Draft order: reverse standings (worst team picks first)
- Teams that were relegated pick in the bottom half of their new league's order

### Draft Class
- Generated by Claude API at the start of each off-season
- Each prospect gets: name, position, overall (raw, 50–75), potential (ceiling rating), 
  a scouting blurb, and a funny/weird personality note
- Prospects develop: +2 to +8 overall over 3 seasons if they pan out

### Draft Picks as Trade Assets
- Future picks (up to 3 years out) can be traded
- Picks are identified as: "2026 3rd round pick — Duluth Foghorns"

---

## Trades

### How Trades Work
You never initiate trades. Rival GMs contact you.

A trade offer arrives as a news item during the season (roughly 1–2 per 5 games).
You see:
- Which team is offering
- Their GM's name and personality blurb
- What they want from you
- What they're offering in return
- A simple value indicator (Claude evaluates and gives a short opinion)

You **Accept** or **Decline**. That's it.

### GM Personalities
Each rival GM has a personality that shapes their offers:

| Personality | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `cheapskate` | Always lowballs. Wants your good players for scrubs. |
| `win_now` | Desperate. Will overpay veterans. Ignores youth. |
| `rebuilder` | Wants prospects and picks. Gives you aging vets. |
| `gambler` | Random chaos. Offers make no logical sense. |
| `hoarder` | Rarely offers. Protective of their good players. |
| `desperate` | Near relegation. Will deal almost anything. |

Personality affects the *tone* of the offer text, not just the value.

## Game Simulation

### How a Game is Simulated (pseudocode)

```
offenseRating = avg(forward overalls) × 0.6 + avg(defence overalls) × 0.4
defenceRating = avg(defence overalls) × 0.6 + avg(forward overalls) × 0.4
goalieRating  = goalie overall

homeBonus = 3 (added to offenseRating for home team)

expectedGoals = (offenseRating - opponentDefenceRating + 30) / 20
  clamped to 0.5–6.0

actualGoals = Poisson sample from expectedGoals, adjusted by goalie rating

If tied after 60 min → OT (coin flip with slight home advantage)
If still tied → SO (coin flip)
```

### Highlights
After each game, 2–3 highlight strings are generated:
- "Boisvert scores shorthanded in the 2nd" 
- "Yablonski stops 38 of 40 shots in a barn-burner"
- "Roughnecks win in OT on a flukey bounce off the referee"

These are template-based with player names injected. NOT Claude API calls
(to keep simming fast). Claude only writes the headline summary.

## In-Game Events

Random events fire during the season (roughly 1 per 3–4 games). They are
generated by Claude API and can be:

### Types
- **Funny** — "Your backup goalie was spotted at a curling tournament in Kapuskasing.
  He returned with a bronze medal and an attitude problem."
- **Roster impact** — "Your top scorer pulled a hamstring at team bowling night.
  Out 2 games." (minor stat effects)
- **League-wide** — "The league has banned visor decorations following an incident
  in Moncton. Teams are confused."
- **Goofy** — "The Sudbury Smelters' arena flooded. Their next two home games
  will be played in a Walmart parking lot."

### Event Effects
Events can have minor mechanical effects:
- Player out N games (1–3)
- Morale boost (small overall bump for 3 games)
- Home ice advantage change for 1 game
- No effect (pure flavour)

## UI Screens

### Dashboard (home screen)
- Your team name, record, league position
- Next 3 games on schedule
- Recent news/headline feed (last 5 items)
- Cap situation: used / total
- Quick-access buttons: Roster · Trades · Draft · Sim

### Roster Screen
- Full 25-player roster table
- Columns: name, pos, overall, age, salary, contract years, trait
- Actions: Release player
- Cap summary at bottom

### Trade Desk
- Pending trade offers (accept / decline)
- Trade history log
- Your tradeable picks listed

### Draft Screen
- Only active during off-season draft phase
- Shows draft board (all 36 picks per round)
- Your picks highlighted
- Prospect card: name, pos, overall, potential, scouting blurb, weird note
- Pick / pass (auto-pick best available if you pass)

### News Feed
- Chronological list of all events, headlines, trade results, game results
- Filterable by: all / games / trades / events / league news

### Sim Controls
- "Sim next game" — simulates one game, generates headline
- "Sim week" — simulates 5 games at once, batch headlines
- "Sim to playoffs" — simulates remaining regular season games
- After each sim: standings update, news items added

## Saving & Loading

- Game state saves to localStorage automatically after every action
- State object:

```js
{
  version: '1.0',
  year: number,
  week: number,           // 1–20 (game number in season)
  phase: 'preseason' | 'season' | 'playoffs' | 'offseason',
  playerTeam: teamId,
  leagues: { phl: [...teams], cd: [...teams], rc: [...teams] },
  allPlayers: { [playerId]: playerObject },
  schedule: [...games],
  results: [...gameResults],
  news: [...newsItems],
  capHistory: [...capByYear],
  draftHistory: [...draftPicks],
  tradeHistory: [...trades]
}
```

## Tone & Style

- Goofy but not stupid. Think minor league hockey energy.
- Rival GMs have names like "Hank Brulé" or "Darcy Spratt"
- Headlines should be deadpan and Canadian: "Wawa Wolverines extend losing streak,
  cite bus breakdown near Chapleau"
- Events should feel like they came from a small-town sports section
- Player traits: "glass jaw", "hot dog", "lunch-pail guy", "locker room cancer",
  "clutch", "playoff beard god", "fights his own shadow"
- Arena names: functional and grim. "Sudbury Community Plex", "The Walmart Barn",
  "Moose Jaw Civic Centre (Sponsored by Grippo's Mufflers)"
